A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Small questions.
Thoughtful answers.

Can I order these gifts?

No. Palm & Pit is a fictional brand created for a portfolio. This website demonstrates branding, visual design and front-end interactions.

What would personalised gifting look like?

The concept explores coordinated sleeves, message cards and occasion-led packaging. These are illustrative ideas, not an available service or production specification.

Is this a real brand?

No. Palm & Pit is a fictional brand created for a design portfolio. The packaging and website are concept work.
